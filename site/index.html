SMALL BY DESIGN. HONEST ABOUT THE EDGES.

It's a tool, not a permission slip.

Short-lived copies

At most ten minutes, or sooner when validity is shorter. Originals stay in your mail and messages. Agent/provider history has its own rules.

Trust the agent you connect

Every reader key can access your captured inbox. Match before claim. A code never authorizes a payment, security change or sharing with someone else.

Personal scale

One owner, one replica, memory storage. A restart resets claims. Capture can miss messages. Keep Gmail polling as backup and watch-renewal driver.

Proof, not promises

The private predecessor handled real iPhone SMS, three Gmail accounts and a real login. This public edition has local tests. Android, Instinct and Muse are recipes awaiting client/device tests.
