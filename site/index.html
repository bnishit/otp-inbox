SMALL BY DESIGN. HONEST ABOUT THE EDGES.

A small tool.
With clear boundaries.

Short-lived copies

At most ten minutes, or sooner when validity is shorter. Originals stay in your mail and messages. Agent/provider history has its own rules.

Trust the agent you connect

Every reader key can access your captured inbox. Match before claim. Retrieval alone does not authorize a payment or security change. Payment filling is a separate saved owner choice; it defaults off.

Personal scale

One owner, one replica, memory storage. A restart resets claims. Capture can miss messages. Keep Gmail polling as backup and watch-renewal driver.

Proof, not promises

The maintainer pilot completed a Blinkit SMS login; the owner reports a successful Amazon purchase OTP test. Local coverage: 87 Bun tests plus synthetic browser checks. Android and native OS banner delivery still need live proof.
